## Where this departs from the draft

- The draft's shared "all levels" questions were replaced with per-level sets, which is
  what all 117 existing articles use.
- The draft's story 1 text contained an em dash and used only a fraction of the source.
  It was rewritten from the full report, which carries far more usable detail: the height
  of the building, the coolers and generators, the Cardiff park and ride campaign, and the
  councillor who reversed his position.
- The holiday story uses two sources via the source-2 field, because the two surveys
  disagree and the disagreement is the story.

## Accuracy decisions

- Data centre: the 315 queued centres and their combined power request are presented as
  applications, not consumption, at all three levels. The developer's own air quality
  position is attributed to the developer.
- Wine: all figures are labelled as ministry estimates rather than results. The quality
  praise is attributed to one named producer describing his own harvest. The reason vines
  were removed is not given in the source, so it is not supplied here.
- Holidays: each figure is tied to its own survey, with samples, countries and dates
  stated. The two are never added together.
- Dinosaur: the classification is attributed to Sotheby's, with the earlier juvenile
  T. rex position noted. The auction price is distinguished from the seller's proceeds at
  Core, Intermediate and Advanced.

## Photo prompts

All four have prompts in tools/photo_prompts.json. The prompt formula was rewritten this
round at John's request: still a commercial editorial stock photo, but much less staged.
One subject and one in-focus detail instead of a prop inventory, available light, an
explicit instruction that the scene should look found rather than built, and arranged
props and styled flat lays added to the Avoid list. The six prompts from 1 November were
rewritten to the same formula. The 77 prompts for stories that already have their pictures
installed were left alone.
