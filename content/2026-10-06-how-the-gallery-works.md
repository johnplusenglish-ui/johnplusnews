This is the second sample article. On the home page, every article appears as a card: a picture, the title and a short line of summary underneath.

The newest article comes first. Each card also shows the level, the date and how long the article takes to read. The tabs above the cards let a learner see only intermediate, advanced or proficient articles.

If an article has no summary written, the site uses the start of the first paragraph instead. If an article has no picture, the card shows its level on a plain coloured block.
