Adding an article is a three step routine.

- Share the article with Claude, along with its source and its level: intermediate, advanced or proficient.
- A new text file is added to the content folder, and the site is rebuilt.
- The updated site is checked locally, then published.

That is all. There is no database, no login and no admin page to maintain, which keeps the site fast and easy to keep running.
