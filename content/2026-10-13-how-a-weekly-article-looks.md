This is a sample article. Its only job is to show how a real article will look on the page, so you can judge the frame before any real content goes in.

Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. You can add a **bold phrase** for emphasis, or a [link to another page](https://johnplusnews.com) inside a sentence.

## A subheading

Longer articles can be broken up with subheadings like the one above. Keep them short, and use them only where the reader needs a signpost.

### A smaller subheading

There is also a second level, for articles with several sections.

Lists work too:

- One thing to notice
- Another thing to notice
- A third thing, a little longer than the others, to show how a line wraps on a narrow phone screen

> A quotation or a key sentence can sit in its own highlighted block like this one.

At the bottom of every article there is an optional source line, and links to the older and newer articles, so a reader can keep going without going back to the menu.
