Pictures

Every article has one picture. Drop a picture onto a card, click it to choose a file, or hover over it and paste. Each card also holds a ready-made photo prompt for that article: press Copy prompt, paste it into your image tool, then drop the result back here. You can change a prompt in its box and press Save prompt to keep your wording. Press Publish pictures once when you are done. The articles' text is never touched.

Many at once: drop several pictures here. A file named like its article (for example fast-fashion-the-clothes-nobody-wants.jpg) goes to that article.
