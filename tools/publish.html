Tags let students find related articles. They show as buttons on the home page and under the article, and the search box finds them too. Reuse a tag you have used before when you can, so related articles stay together. Press a tag below to add it. Press a tag above to remove it. Up to 8 tags.
Picture
This is how the gallery card crops it.
Shown as a small line under the picture on the article page, for example "Photo: Barcex, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons, resized". For a picture made with AI, put Image in the first-word box and AI-generated as the credit. The name and the licence become links if you give links. A credit does not give you permission to use a picture, so only use pictures you are allowed to publish.
Levels and article text
Tick each level you are adding a version for, then paste that version's text below. If you tick more than one, readers get a tab for each level on the article page. Each level can have its own headline and subheading; leave them empty to use the title and summary above. A blank line starts a new paragraph. Use ## for a heading, **bold**, a dash and a space for list items, and > for a quote.
Questions (optional)
Readers see these as two quiet rows that open on request, one above the article text and one below it, and both are printed in the PDF. Write one question per line. Every level shares the same questions, so keep the wording simple enough for the Core level.
