About

johnplusnews publishes real news stories rewritten for English learners, so that a learner can read about something worth talking about at a level that suits them.

Who makes it

johnplusnews is an independent English-learning publication, written and edited by John, an English teacher. It is part of johnplusenglish.

The three levels

Every story is written three times. They tell the same story, with different English.

  • CoreShort sentences and everyday words.
  • IntermediateLonger sentences and more detail.
  • AdvancedNatural language, richer detail and room for discussion.

Choose a level with the buttons at the top of any article. The reading, the questions and the exercises all follow the level you choose. These are our own levels, written by eye for the learners we teach. They have not been formally calibrated against the CEFR, so we do not publish an A2 or B1 label.

Where the stories come from

Each article starts from a published report by a news organisation. The source is named and linked at the foot of every article, with its date, so you can read the original. We rewrite the story in our own words for learners; we do not republish anyone else’s text.

How we use AI

We are open about this because it affects how much you should trust what you read.

  • The writing is drafted with AI assistance and then edited by John. Every article carries that note.
  • The images are generated for this site. They are illustrations, not photographs of the events reported, and each one is labelled AI-generated image under the picture. Where a story is about a real named person, the image does not claim to show them.
  • The facts are the part AI is worst at, so the figures, names and dates in an article should match the source we link to. If one does not, that is a mistake and we want to hear about it.

Saving words

Select any word or phrase while you read and it is saved to My words, with the sentence you found it in. With a free account your words follow you between devices, and a teacher you have joined can see the words you save and the articles you read. Nobody else can.

Corrections

If something is wrong, please tell us and we will correct it.
