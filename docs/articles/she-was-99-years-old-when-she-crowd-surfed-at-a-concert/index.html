9Role-play: a special day out for Gran

Work in pairs. Read your card, then have the conversation. Swap roles and do it again.

Student A · the grandchild

You want to take your grandmother, who is eighty-five, to a live concert. She has said she would love to go. Explain your plan and answer your parent's worries.

Student B · the parent

You love your mother, but you are worried that a noisy concert will be too tiring. Ask at least two questions. You can say yes, no, or yes with a condition.

Useful phrases

  • I'd like to take Gran to a live concert.
  • She's really excited about it.
  • I'm worried it'll be too tiring for her.
  • Let's ask her what she wants.
  • How about we book seats near the exit?
  • I'm sure she'll be fine if we plan it carefully.

Afterwards, tell the class: did the parent agree, and what condition made the plan safe?
