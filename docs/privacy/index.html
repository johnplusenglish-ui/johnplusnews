Privacy notice

Last updated 19 October 2026

This notice explains what johnplusnews does with your information. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who we are

johnplusnews is run by John, trading as CorkEnglishTeacher in Ireland. We are the controller of the personal information described here. You can reach us on the contact page.

What we collect

You can read the free articles without an account. To read the others you need a free account. If you create one, we collect:

  • your name and email address;
  • your password, which is handled by our sign-in provider and stored in scrambled form, so we cannot read it;
  • the words and phrases you highlight, with the sentence each one came from;
  • which articles you have opened or finished, and the level you read;
  • your preferences: reading font, level and timer length.

Without an account, your highlights and preferences stay in your own browser and are not sent to us.

Why we use it

To run your account and give you the features you asked for: saving words, remembering your place, and sending sign-in and password emails. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

Your teacher, who runs this site, can see your name, email, the date you joined, when you were last active, your saved words, and which articles you have opened or finished. This is the point of the student view. Nobody else can see it. The sign-up form tells you this before you create an account.

Cookies and similar storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics. The site stores a small amount of information in your browser that it needs to work: your sign-in, your highlights and your preferences. This is strictly necessary for the features you use, so we do not show a cookie banner. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings, or by signing out.

Services we use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any host, it receives your internet address when you load a page.
  • Supabase provides the database and sign-in for accounts.
  • Resend delivers the emails we send you.

The fonts are hosted on this site, so your device does not contact Google. Some of these providers are based outside Ireland. We choose providers that publish their own data protection terms.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. If you delete your account, your account information, saved words and progress are deleted from our live database. Copies may remain in provider backups for a short time.

Your rights

You can ask to see your information, correct it, take a copy, or have it deleted. You can download your saved words from the My words page, and delete your account there too. For anything else, contact us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Children

johnplusnews is written for adult learners. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top.
