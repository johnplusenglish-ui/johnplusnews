How it works

Everything you can do on johnplusnews, in the order you’ll need it. It takes about three minutes to read.

  1. 1Create your free account
  2. 2Find a story
  3. 3Choose your level
  4. 4Read and listen
  5. 5Save the words you want to keep
  6. 6Do the lesson
  7. 7Go back over your words
  8. 8Your account and your privacy

1Create your free account

  • Press Sign in at the top of any page, then Create an account. The box also opens by itself when you arrive.
  • Type your name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters, then press Create account.
  • We send you an email. Open it and press the link to confirm your address. If it isn't there after a few minutes, look in your spam folder.
  • Now sign in with your email and password. Your first name appears at the top right, and that's it: every article is open to you.

Forgotten your password? Press Sign in, then Forgotten your password?. We email you a link. It works once, for about an hour, and you need to open it in the same browser you asked from.

2Find a story

  • Articles lists every story, newest first. The topic buttons narrow the list down.
  • Teens has stories for learners aged 12 to 18.
  • Once you're signed in, the cards say In progress or Completed, so you can see where you stopped.
  • No account yet? Three stories are free to read.

3Choose your level

Every story is written three times. It's the same story, with different English.

  • Core uses short sentences and everyday words. It's about B1.
  • Intermediate has longer sentences and more detail. It's about B2.
  • Advanced is natural English with richer detail. It's about C1 to C2.
  • Press a level at the top of the article. The text, the tasks and the worksheet all change with it.

Not sure which to choose? Start with Intermediate. If it feels hard, move down a level. If it feels easy, move up.

4Read and listen

  • Many stories have a Listen bar. Press play to hear the article read aloud.
  • The 1x button changes the speed. In the same menu, turn on Follow the words to see each word light up as it's spoken.
  • The paragraphs are numbered, so a class can talk about paragraph 3 without anyone getting lost.

5Save the words you want to keep

  • Select a word or phrase in the article. A Highlight button appears next to it. Press it. On a computer, Alt+H does the same.
  • Tap a highlighted word to see what it means, or to remove it.
  • Your words gather under Your words at the bottom of the page, each with the sentence you found it in. Write the meaning your own way in the Say it your way box: it's the best way to remember it.

Select inside one paragraph at a time. A word or a short phrase works best.

6Do the lesson

Each story is a lesson in four stages, with nine numbered tasks.

  • Before you read. 1 Warm up, 2 Key words.
  • Read. 3 The article.
  • After you read. 4 True, false or not given, 5 Vocabulary, 6 Verb forms or Word forms, 7 Language focus.
  • Speak. 8 Talk about it, 9 a role-play or activity for pairs.
  • Press a task to open it. Press Check answers to see your score, and Try again to clear it and start again. Open all opens tasks 4 to 7 together.
  • Press Worksheet PDF to download the same lesson to print. The answers are on the last page.

Teachers: the Timer at the top of most articles counts down an activity and chimes at zero. A few older stories don't have the lesson layout yet, and their exercises are under Study this story at the bottom.

7Go back over your words

  • Open My words from the menu under your name.
  • Every word you've saved is there, grouped by article. Use Search your words to find one.
  • Open a word to see its sentence and meaning, add your own note, or Remove it.
  • Download CSV saves the list for a spreadsheet or a flashcard app.

8Your account and your privacy

  • Your teacher, who runs this site, can see your name, your email, the words you save and look up, and the articles you open or finish. Nobody else can. The privacy notice has the details.
  • Sign out is in the menu under your name.
  • To delete your account and every word you've saved, open My words and press Delete my account at the bottom. This can't be undone.

If something goes wrong

The confirmation email hasn't come.

Look in your spam folder. Still nothing? Try to sign in: you'll see a Send that email again button.

The link says it has expired.

Links work once, for about an hour. Ask for a new one and open it in the same browser.

An article says it's for members.

Create a free account and sign in, and every article opens.

I can't see my words on another device.

Check that you're signed in there with the same email. Your words follow your account, not your device.

Still stuck? Tell us what happened and we’ll sort it out.
